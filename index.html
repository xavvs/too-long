Republic Act No. 11261

First-Time Jobseekers Assistance Act Hub

Fresh graduates, out-of-school youth, and first-time workers are entitled to waived government fees on essential pre-employment documents when presenting a free Barangay First-Time Jobseeker Certification.

STEP 1 • START HERE
Get Your Barangay Certification (FREE)

Before visiting NBI, PNP, PSA, or BIR, go to your local Barangay Hall first.

  • Eligibility: Filipino citizen, residing in the barangay for at least 6 months, actively seeking employment for the first time (fresh grad, K-12 completer, or out-of-school youth).
  • What to Bring: Valid ID, School ID / Diploma / Transcript of Records, and proof of 6-month residency.
  • Sign the Oath of Undertaking: Executed free at the Barangay Hall (no notarization required).
  • Validity: Valid for 1 year from issuance; usable once per agency.
Bring photocopies of your Barangay Certificate & Oath of Undertaking to each agency!
100% FEE-WAIVED (₱0)
Covered FREE Documents

Present your Barangay Certificate + Oath of Undertaking to waive fees for:

  • NBI Clearance (₱130 fee waived)
  • PNP National Police Clearance (₱150 fee waived)
  • Barangay Clearance & Cedula (LGU fees waived)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (1 copy free at walk-in CRS)
  • PSA Marriage Certificate (1 copy free at walk-in CRS)
  • BIR TIN Registration (Form 1902/1904 & TIN card free)
  • Public Hospital Medical Certificate (doctor's fee waived; lab tests excluded)
  • Transcript of Records (TOR) & Diploma from SUCs/LUCs
  • SSS, PhilHealth & Pag-IBIG initial member registration
EXEMPTED UNDER LAW
Not Covered (Regular Fees Apply)

Under Section 5 of RA 11261, the fee waiver does NOT apply to the following:

  • Philippine Passport (DFA): Regular ₱950 / ₱1,200 fee applies, including DFA Apostille authentication.
  • LTO Driver's License & Student Permit: Regular LTO licensing, medical, and driving school fees apply.
  • PRC Board Exams & Professional ID: Licensure exam and registration fees are not covered.
  • Civil Service Commission (CSC) Exams: Career Service Exam filing fees apply.
  • PSA Online Courier Delivery: Walk-in PSA CRS is free, but private courier fees (PSAHelpline/Serbilis) are paid by applicant.
Refused by Barangay? Escalate to your City/Municipal Mayor's Office, PESO, or DILG Field Office.

RA 10932 (Anti-Hospital Deposit Law): It is ILLEGAL for any hospital or clinic in the Philippines to demand any deposit or advance payment before administering emergency medical treatment to stabilize a patient.
1. What to Prepare Before Admission
  • Patient Info Sheet: Full name, birthdate, blood type, known allergies, current medications, and pre-existing conditions.
  • Valid Government ID: Patient's valid ID and the companion/watcher's valid ID.
  • PhilHealth / HMO Details: PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), Member Data Record (MDR), or HMO card/LOA.
  • Emergency Cash & Charger: Small bills for initial medicines/lab receipts outside ER stock, plus phone charger.
2. Emergency Admission Process
  1. ER Triage: Proceed straight to the Emergency Room triage nurse for vital signs and urgency assessment.
  2. Stabilization First: Doctors stabilize life-threatening symptoms immediately without upfront payment.
  3. ER Registration: Companion registers the patient at the Admitting/ER desk using valid IDs.
  4. Ward / Room Admission: Once cleared for admission, sign consent forms and coordinate PhilHealth/Malasakit Center endorsement.
3. PhilHealth & Medical Aid Docs
  • PhilHealth Claim Form 1 (CF1): Signed by member or authorized representative.
  • Updated MDR: Obtainable at the hospital's PhilHealth/Malasakit desk.
  • For Zero-Balance Billing (Public Hospitals): Indigent, Senior Citizen, PWD, or Kasambahay status + Malasakit Center intake.

1. Emergency Reporting (BFP)

Call 911 or your local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) station immediately. Do not assume someone else has called.

  • State the exact street address & barangay.
  • Provide nearby landmarks (schools, churches, malls).
  • Describe what is burning (house, vehicle, electrical post) and if anyone is trapped.
  • Stay on the line until the dispatcher confirms details.
Call 911 / BFP Dispatch
2. Fire Safety Procedures (PASS)

Only fight a small, contained fire if your escape path is clear behind you. Use a fire extinguisher with P.A.S.S.:

  • P - Pull the safety pin at the top of the extinguisher.
  • A - Aim the nozzle at the base of the fire, not the flames.
  • S - Squeeze the lever slowly and evenly.
  • S - Sweep from side to side until the fire is completely out.
  • Never throw water on grease/cooking oil or live electrical fires!
3. Smoke & Evacuation Tips
  • Get Low and Go: Crawl below thick smoke where cleaner, cooler air stays near the floor.
  • Check Doors First: Touch closed doors with the back of your hand. If hot, do not open—use an alternate exit or window.
  • Never Use Elevators: Always use emergency stairwells.
  • Stop, Drop, and Roll: If clothes catch fire, cover your face and roll on the ground to smother flames.
  • Never Re-enter: Stay at your designated assembly area until BFP declares the structure safe.

Disaster Preparedness Center

Situated along the Pacific Ring of Fire and Typhoon Belt, every Filipino household needs a ready plan. Review hazard protocols and track your 72-hour Emergency Go-Bag below.

Typhoon Preparedness (PAGASA Signals)

Monitor official DOST-PAGASA bulletins every 6 hours. Understand Tropical Cyclone Wind Signals (TCWS):

Signal Wind Speed Action Required
TCWS #139–61 km/hSecure loose roofs/objects; monitor advisories.
TCWS #262–88 km/hClasses suspended; avoid sea travel; charge devices.
TCWS #389–117 km/hEvacuate low-lying/coastal areas; stay indoors away from glass.
TCWS #4–5118–185+ km/hCatastrophic winds. Shelter in strongest interior room or evacuation center.
After the Typhoon: Watch out for fallen power lines, contaminated tap water, and weakened structures before stepping outside.
Flood Preparedness & Leptospirosis Prevention
  • PAGASA Rainfall Warnings: Yellow (Monitor), Orange (Alert - Flooding threatening), Red (Emergency - Serious flooding; evacuate immediately).
  • Turn Off Utilities: Switch off the main electrical breaker and close LPG gas tanks before floodwaters enter your home.
  • Never Walk or Drive Through Floods: Just 15 cm (6 inches) of fast-moving water can knock an adult down; 60 cm (2 feet) can sweep away vehicles.
  • Avoid Leptospirosis: If wading through floodwater is unavoidable, wear boots and wash thoroughly with soap afterward. Visit a Barangay Health Center within 24 hours for free Doxycycline prophylaxis.
Earthquake Safety: DROP, COVER, and HOLD
1. DROP
Drop onto your hands and knees before shaking knocks you down.
2. COVER
Cover your head & neck under a sturdy table or against an interior wall.
3. HOLD ON
Hold on to your shelter until all shaking completely stops.
  • If Outdoors: Move to an open area away from buildings, trees, streetlights, and utility wires.
  • Tsunami Warning: If you are near the coast and feel strong shaking lasting 20+ seconds, move to higher ground immediately without waiting for official alerts.
Volcanic Eruption & Ashfall Response (PHIVOLCS)
  • Respect Permanent Danger Zones (PDZ): Never enter the 4km–6km PDZ of active volcanoes (Taal, Mayon, Kanlaon, Bulusan).
  • Protect Against Ashfall: Wear an N95 mask (or damp cloth) and goggles. Keep doors and windows closed; place damp towels at door gaps.
  • Protect Vehicles & Electronics: Do not drive in heavy ashfall (ash clogs air filters and scratches windshields). Cover computers and appliances.
  • Clear Roof Ash Safely: Wet ash is heavy and can collapse roofs. Sweep it off cautiously once eruption activity subsides.
Pre-Emptive Evacuation Guidelines
  • Evacuate Early: Leave immediately when your Barangay DRRMC issues a pre-emptive or forced evacuation order—do not wait until roads are impassable.
  • Bring Your Go-Bag & IDs: Carry waterproofed documents, maintenance medicines, and infant/elderly supplies.
  • Register at the Evacuation Center: Log your family members with the DSWD / Barangay desk so you are accounted for and receive relief assistance.
  • Family Reunification Plan: Agree on an out-of-area relative as a central contact person in case local cellular networks get congested.
